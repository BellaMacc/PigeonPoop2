How to play:

Launch the Prototype Submission scene


Controls:

For Keyboard
Use WASD to move around
E to flap your wings
Right Mouse Click to poop
Mouse movement to move camera

For Controller
Left Joystick to move around
Right Joystick for camera movement
Right Trigger to flap wings
West button (xbox controller X) to Poop

In the prototype, you can poop 5 times
